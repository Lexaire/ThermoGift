Rules

Fill thermometers from the round bulb toward the rounded tip. A later segment cannot be filled unless every segment before it is filled. The numbers above and beside the grid tell how many red cells belong in that column or row. Right-click a cell to mark an X. When every count matches and the thermometers obey the rule, the solved grid unlocks the giveaway code.
